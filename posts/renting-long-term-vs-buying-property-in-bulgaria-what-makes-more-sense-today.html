Renting Long-Term vs. Buying Property in Bulgaria: What Makes More Sense Today?

peak_seeker
Topic Starter
Posted: 31.01.2025 12:20
I am currently living in Sofia and debating whether to continue renting or to finally invest in my own apartment. With the recent fluctuations in interest rates and the significant price growth in major cities like Plovdiv and Varna, I am wondering if the market is currently overpriced. Has anyone navigated this decision recently, and do you feel that mortgage payments are currently outweighing the cost of renting long-term? I would appreciate any advice on whether it is better to wait for a potential market correction or lock in a property now.
