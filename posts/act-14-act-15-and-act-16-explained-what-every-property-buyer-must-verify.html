Act 14, Act 15, and Act 16 Explained: What Every Property Buyer Must Verify

quartz_vibe
Topic Starter

I am currently in the final stages of closing on my first property and have been hearing a lot of conflicting advice regarding the mandatory verification of Acts 14, 15, and 16. Could someone please clarify what these specific legal provisions entail and why they are so critical for a buyer's protection? I want to ensure I am not missing any red flags before I sign the final deed. Any advice or experiences from recent buyers would be greatly appreciated.

Act 14 is the structural shell, Act 15 confirms the building is complete, and Act 16 is the final permission to use. Without Act 16, you literally cannot legally live in the property. It is non-negotiable.
