Living in Bansko Year-Round: Is it really a digital nomad haven during the off-season?

echo_point
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.01.2025 10:07
I've been looking into moving to Bansko for a few months and am curious about the reality of staying there outside of the peak ski season. Does the town completely shut down once the snow melts, or is there still a thriving digital nomad community to connect with? I'm specifically interested in the quality of co-working spaces and the general atmosphere during the quiet summer and autumn months. Any insights from full-time residents or long-term nomads would be greatly appreciated!

Bansko is actually better in the summer! The town doesn't shut down; it just shifts from ski boots to hiking boots. The nomad community is very active year-round thanks to events like Bansko Nomad Fest. You'll find plenty of people in the coworking spaces even in October.

Don't worry about the town 'shutting down.' Many restaurants stay open, and the local expat community is super welcoming. It's a great place to focus on work without the distractions of a big city.
