Property Maintenance Fees in Bulgarian Resorts: What Is Normal and How to Avoid Overpaying?

wild_tether
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.01.2025 07:28
I am currently looking at buying a vacation apartment in Sunny Beach and have been shocked by the wide range of maintenance fees quoted by different complexes. Some are charging 8 EUR per square meter, while others are asking for nearly double that amount without a clear breakdown of services. What is considered a reasonable annual fee in the current market, and what specific costs should be included in that price? I would also appreciate any advice on how to negotiate these fees or protect myself from aggressive annual hikes after the purchase.

In Sunny Beach, 8-12 EUR per square meter is the standard range. Anything above 15 EUR is usually a premium complex with multiple pools and year-round security.
