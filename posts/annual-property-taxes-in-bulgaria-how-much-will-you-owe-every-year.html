Annual Property Taxes in Bulgaria: How Much Will You Owe Every Year?

arctic_nomad
Topic Starter
Posted: 07.01.2025 09:44
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday apartment in the Varna region and I am trying to budget for the long-term ownership costs. I have heard conflicting information about how annual property taxes are calculated and whether they vary significantly between municipalities. Could someone explain the current tax rates and if there are any exemptions for foreign owners? I would appreciate any advice on how to estimate these annual liabilities before I finalize my decision.

Property taxes in Varna are quite reasonable compared to Western Europe. Generally, they range between 0.1% and 0.25% of the tax valuation, which is determined by the municipality. If you are serious about relocating, check out this guide on the real cost of living in Bulgaria to get a full picture of your monthly expenses.

Don't forget that besides the property tax, you also have to pay a garbage collection fee, which is often higher than the tax itself. It varies by municipality, so check the specific rate for the Varna district before you sign anything.

As a foreigner, you don't get any special tax exemptions compared to locals. You pay the exact same rates. If you need help navigating the legal side of things, read this guide on buying property in Bulgaria to avoid common pitfalls.

The tax valuation of your property is usually lower than the market price, which is good news for your wallet. Just ensure the apartment has 'Act 16' status, or your utility bills and tax assessments could become a headache.

I own a place in the Varna region. The tax is calculated based on the location and the type of building. It really isn't a massive annual expense, but make sure you register the property at the local tax office within two months of purchasing.

Rates are set annually by the Varna Municipal Council. You can find the exact percentage on the municipality's official website, but it usually stays stable for several years at a time.
