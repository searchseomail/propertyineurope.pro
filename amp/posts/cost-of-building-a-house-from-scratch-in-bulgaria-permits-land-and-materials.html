Building in rural Bulgaria has become significantly more expensive since 2022 due to the spike in steel and concrete prices. For a modern home, expect to pay between €800 and €1,100 per square meter for 'turnkey' finishing. Permits and architectural designs will add another €3,000–€5,000 depending on the complexity of the project.

Don't underestimate the bureaucracy. Getting the 'Razreshenie za stroezh' (building permit) can take anywhere from 6 to 12 months. My advice is to hire a local architect who has a good relationship with the municipal authorities, as they can navigate the paperwork much faster than a foreigner can.

Construction costs have jumped by roughly 25-30% over the last two years. Materials like insulation and high-quality windows have seen the biggest increases. Budgeting for a buffer of 20% over your initial quote is essential because contractors often hit 'unexpected' snags in rural areas.

The 'price per square meter' is a bit of a trap. A shell ('Act 14') will cost you around €400–€500 per square meter, but the finishing works—tiles, bathrooms, kitchen, and heating systems—will easily double that. Don't cheap out on the insulation; winters in rural Bulgaria can be quite harsh.
