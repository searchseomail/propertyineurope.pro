Are Bulgarian Property Management Companies Worth the Fees? Reviews and Alternatives

cipher_mist
Topic Starter
Posted: 26.06.2025 11:51
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday apartment in Sunny Beach and am weighing whether to sign up with a professional property management firm for rentals and maintenance. The fees seem to eat up a significant portion of potential income, and I am worried about the quality of service provided. Has anyone had positive or negative experiences with these companies, and are there viable alternatives for managing a property remotely? I would appreciate any honest reviews or advice from current owners in Bulgaria.

Honestly, avoid the big management companies in Sunny Beach if you can. They take 20-30% commissions, and the maintenance quality is often subpar. Before you commit, make sure you understand the real risks of buying cheap apartments in that area.
fable_runner

I managed my place remotely for two seasons. It was a nightmare. I ended up hiring a local independent cleaner who is much cheaper and more reliable than the complex management. You need a trusted local contact on the ground.
wild_tether
Replied: 23.07.2025 02:36
The fees are definitely high, but they handle the keys, cleaning, and guest check-ins. If you don't live in Bulgaria, doing it yourself is almost impossible unless you have a very reliable friend nearby.
