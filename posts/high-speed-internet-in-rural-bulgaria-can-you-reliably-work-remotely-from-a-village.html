High-Speed Internet in Rural Bulgaria: Can You Reliably Work Remotely from a Village?

dusk_pilot
Topic Starter
Posted: 04.02.2025 12:34
I am planning to move from Sofia to a smaller village in the Rhodope Mountains to enjoy a quieter lifestyle, but I need a stable connection for daily Zoom calls and large file uploads. Has anyone here successfully transitioned to full-time remote work while living in rural Bulgaria, and which ISPs would you recommend? I am worried about whether fiber optic coverage actually reaches these remote areas or if I should look into Starlink instead. Any advice on checking real-world speeds before signing a long-term lease would be greatly appreciated.

The biggest issue in the mountains isn't just the ISP, it's the power grid. Winter storms can cut your connection. Invest in a good UPS and a 4G backup dongle.

I've been working from a village near Smolyan for two years. Local ISPs are often better than the national giants in these areas because they care more about the local customer base.

Fiber in the Rhodopes is rare. Most villages run on directional wireless internet (WISP). It’s stable, but you need a clear line of sight to the transmitter.
