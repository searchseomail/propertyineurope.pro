Buying Commercial Property in Bulgaria: Small Retail Spaces vs. Office Investments

urban_echo
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.01.2025 04:06
I am currently looking into diversifying my portfolio by investing in Bulgarian commercial real estate, specifically focusing on Sofia and Plovdiv. I am torn between purchasing small ground-floor retail units in high-traffic residential areas versus investing in modern office spaces in business districts. Given the current market trends and post-pandemic shifts in working habits, which asset class offers better long-term stability and rental yields? I would appreciate insights from anyone with experience navigating the Bulgarian commercial property market regarding liquidity and tenant retention.

Retail units in high-traffic residential areas of Sofia, like Lozenets or Manastirski Livadi, are generally more resilient. People will always need grocery stores and pharmacies nearby, regardless of economic shifts.
