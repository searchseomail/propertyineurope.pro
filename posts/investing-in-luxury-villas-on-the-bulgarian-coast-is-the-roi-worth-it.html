Investing in Luxury Villas on the Bulgarian Coast: Is the ROI Worth It?

haven_quest
Topic Starter
Posted: 17.02.2025 16:01
I am currently looking into purchasing a premium villa along the Bulgarian Black Sea coast, specifically in areas like Sozopol or near Varna. I am concerned about the long-term build quality of newer developments and whether these properties hold their resale value in the current market. Could anyone share their experiences regarding maintenance costs and the potential for capital appreciation in these exclusive locations?

Regarding resale value, Sozopol is much more stable than the chaotic development areas near Sunny Beach. It has a permanent soul, which helps retain value. Just be prepared for high maintenance fees for gated communities.

Varna is a city that lives year-round, whereas the coast near Sozopol can feel like a ghost town in winter. If you want ROI, Varna is safer for long-term rentals, but Sozopol is better for high-end summer Airbnb income.
