I hate to be the bearer of bad news, but simply buying a house does not grant you a D visa or residency in Bulgaria. You still need a legal basis to stay, like being a pensioner, a company owner, or having a specific job. You should definitely look into investing in Bulgarian real estate as a personal asset, but don't count on it as your ticket to a visa.

That is a common myth. Real estate ownership is not a direct path to residency. You have to qualify through other means, such as opening a trade representative office or starting a local business. The property is just a nice place to live once you secure your legal status.
