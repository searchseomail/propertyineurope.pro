wild_tether
Topic Starter

I am currently looking to diversify my portfolio by investing in the Sofia property market and would love some local insight. Which neighborhoods currently offer the best balance between long-term capital appreciation and stable rental yields for residential apartments? I am particularly interested in how the ongoing infrastructure developments and the metro expansion are influencing property values in emerging areas. Any advice on potential pitfalls for foreign investors would also be greatly appreciated.
