copper_fern
Topic Starter
Posted: 28.01.2025 15:42
I am currently looking into purchasing a holiday apartment in Bansko as an investment property. While the winter ski season seems like a guaranteed source of income, I am struggling to find data on whether properties remain profitable during the summer months. Has anyone successfully managed year-round rentals in the area, or is the market strictly limited to the winter season? I would appreciate any insights on occupancy rates and the feasibility of maintaining a steady cash flow throughout the year.
