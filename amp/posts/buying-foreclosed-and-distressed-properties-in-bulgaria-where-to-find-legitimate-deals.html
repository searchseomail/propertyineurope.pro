The best place to start is the official Chamber of Private Enforcement Agents website at sales.bcpea.org. It is the only place where all legitimate public auctions are published. Be very careful with third-party sites that claim to have 'exclusive' deals; they are often just scraping the official portal.

As a foreign investor, you really need a local lawyer. The auction process involves a 10% deposit of the starting price, and if you win, you have a very strict deadline to pay the remainder. If you miss it, you lose your deposit. It is not for the faint of heart.
