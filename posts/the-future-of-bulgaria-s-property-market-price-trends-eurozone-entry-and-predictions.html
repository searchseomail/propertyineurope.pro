The Future of Bulgaria's Property Market: Price Trends, Eurozone Entry, and Predictions

opal_circuit
Topic Starter
Posted: 10.01.2025 11:23
I have been monitoring the Bulgarian real estate market for a while and noticed prices in cities like Sofia and Plovdiv are still climbing despite global economic uncertainty. With Bulgaria aiming for Eurozone accession in the near future, I am curious how this transition might impact mortgage rates and overall housing affordability. Are we looking at a potential bubble, or is this sustained growth driven by genuine demand? I would love to hear your predictions on whether now is a good time to invest or if it is wiser to wait for a correction.
