How to Sell an Apartment in Sunny Beach: Pricing Strategies and Fast-Sale Realities

canyon_haze
Topic Starter
Posted: 19.01.2025 08:51
I am looking to sell my holiday apartment in Sunny Beach but I'm struggling to determine a competitive listing price given the current market saturation. I have heard conflicting advice about whether to price high for negotiation or lower to attract a quick cash buyer. What are the realistic expectations for closing times in this region, and are there specific months that are better for attracting serious interest? I would appreciate any advice from those who have successfully navigated the Bulgarian property market recently.

The market in Sunny Beach is heavily saturated right now. If you want a quick sale, price it 5-10% below the lowest comparable listing. Cash buyers are looking for deals, not negotiation room.

I sold mine last year. I listed high to allow for negotiation, but it sat on the market for six months. Eventually, I had to drop the price anyway. Start competitive if you're serious about selling.

Timing is key. The best interest comes between March and May before the season starts. Once July hits, buyers are already focused on their holidays, not paperwork.
