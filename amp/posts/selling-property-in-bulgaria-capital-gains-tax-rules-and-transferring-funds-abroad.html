I am planning to sell my holiday apartment in Sunny Beach and am confused about the current Bulgarian tax regulations regarding capital gains. I would like to know if I am exempt from tax if I have owned the property for more than five years and what documentation the notary requires. Furthermore, what is the most efficient process for transferring the proceeds back to my home bank account in the UK without triggering issues with anti-money laundering regulations? Any advice or experiences from fellow expats would be greatly appreciated.

Yes, you are generally exempt from capital gains tax in Bulgaria if you have owned the property for more than five years. However, you must prove this ownership period. The notary will need your original title deed (Notarial Deed), a tax assessment certificate from the local municipality, and a certificate stating there are no outstanding debts on the property. As for the transfer, keep your Notarial Deed and the bank transfer confirmation from the sale safe. Regarding the transfer to the UK, I found that using a specialized currency exchange service was cheaper than a direct bank-to-bank transfer. Check if you are considered a tax resident in Bulgaria or the UK. If you are a non-resident, the exemption still applies based on the Bulgarian Personal Income Tax Act. For the transfer, provide your UK bank with the notarized sale agreement. They just want to see that the money came from a legitimate property sale, not illicit activity.

I sold my Sunny Beach holiday home in 2022. The notary process was smooth, but be prepared for the 'tax assessment' value to be lower than your sale price. The notary will calculate the fees based on the higher of the two. Regarding the money, a direct SWIFT transfer from a Bulgarian bank account to your UK account is the most transparent path for anti-money laundering purposes.
