Plovdiv Real Estate Market: Why Bulgaria's Culture Capital Is Attracting Investors

solar_tide
Topic Starter
Posted: 02.02.2025 07:50
I've been looking into diversifying my portfolio and keep hearing buzz about the real estate market in Plovdiv. With its status as a cultural hub and a growing tech sector, it seems like a prime spot for long-term growth compared to the saturated market in Sofia. Has anyone here invested in Plovdiv recently, and what are your thoughts on the potential for rental yields versus capital appreciation? I'm curious to hear from locals or expats about which neighborhoods offer the best balance of value and demand.
