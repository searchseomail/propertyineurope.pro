Buying Property in Nessebar: Year-Round Living in New Town vs. Old Town

shadow_weaver
Topic Starter
Posted: 06.02.2025 13:33
I am seriously considering purchasing an apartment in Nessebar to live in year-round, but I am torn between the Old Town and the New Town areas. While the Old Town has incredible charm and history, I am concerned about accessibility and infrastructure during the quiet winter months. Conversely, the New Town seems more practical for daily life, but I am worried about losing the authentic atmosphere. Does anyone have experience living in either area permanently, and which would you recommend for a long-term resident?

Replies (13)

orbit_bound
Replied: 26.02.2025 12:05
I have lived in the New Town for three years now. It is definitely the better choice for year-round living. You have access to schools, banks, and supermarkets that stay open all winter, which makes life much easier. Just make sure you verify the legal status of your purchase, specifically the Act 16 documentation to ensure the building is fully compliant.
