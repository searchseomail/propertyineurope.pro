Heating Costs in Bulgaria During Winter: AC, Pellets, or Central Heating?

cipher_mist
Topic Starter
Posted: 09.01.2025 04:59
I am moving into a new apartment in Sofia and am trying to decide which heating method will be the most cost-effective for the upcoming winter season. I have heard mixed reviews about the efficiency of central heating versus using high-end inverter air conditioners or switching to a pellet stove. Could anyone share their recent monthly experiences regarding electricity bills and fuel prices? I would appreciate advice on which option offers the best balance between comfort and affordability for a standard two-bedroom flat.

Replies (14)

Replied: 12.01.2025 05:54
I live in a 2-bedroom in Lozenets and stick with central heating (Toplofikatsiya). It’s not the cheapest, but the comfort of having the whole apartment warm is unmatched. My bill averages about 150-200 BGN during the peak of winter.
atlas_notes
Replied: 03.02.2025 09:04
If you want to save money, high-end inverter ACs are the way to go. I use Daikin units and my electricity bill rarely exceeds 120 BGN even in January. Just make sure your insulation is decent.

Pellet stoves are great for houses, but for a flat in Sofia, they are a nightmare. You have nowhere to store the bags, and the dust is annoying. Stick to electricity or central heating.
