Is buying property in Sozopol still a smart investment in 2024?

glass_horizon
Topic Starter
Posted: 29.01.2025 07:03
I am seriously considering purchasing a holiday home in Sozopol, as I absolutely love the historic atmosphere of the Old Town. However, I am worried that the massive summer crowds and the current inflated property prices might outweigh the benefits. Has anyone here bought property in the area recently, and do you feel the rental yield justifies the high entry cost? I would love to hear your experiences regarding the trade-off between the town's charm and the seasonal noise.

Replies (9)

ember_quest
Replied: 18.02.2025 08:47
I bought a small apartment in Sozopol back in 2021. While the Old Town is magical in May and September, the peak of July and August is chaotic. If you are looking for high rental yields, you really need to be aggressive with your marketing, but keep in mind that annual property taxes in Bulgaria are relatively low, which helps the bottom line.
nova_sketch
Replied: 04.03.2025 01:48
The prices in Sozopol are definitely inflated right now. I opted to buy a bit further out in the newer part of town. It’s quieter, more spacious, and I still get the rental income during the peak season without the noise of the tourists right outside my window.
copper_fern
Replied: 06.04.2025 02:13
Honestly, if you want a holiday home, look at the lifestyle benefits first. The rental yield is tricky because the season is so short. If you are also thinking about long-term relocation, you might want to look into considering Bulgaria for retirement, as the town is lovely once the summer rush dies down.
breeze_vector
Replied: 02.06.2025 00:48
I sold my place in the Old Town last year. The noise was just too much for me personally. If you aren't going to be there yourself during the peak weeks to manage it, the maintenance costs and wear and tear from renters can really eat into your profits.
misty_anchor
Replied: 02.07.2025 00:08
I think it’s a smart investment only if you find a property that needs some TLC and you're willing to manage the renovation yourself. Just be careful with costs, as buying a rural village house in Bulgaria is often cheaper, but coastal renovations in historic districts come with strict regulations.
terra_byte
Replied: 17.11.2025 09:43
The charm is undeniable, but it comes at a premium. I bought a place two years ago and I mostly use it for my family. We rent it out for just three weeks in August to cover the yearly expenses, and that works perfectly for us without the headache of constant turnovers.
wild_tether
Replied: 21.11.2025 02:04
Rental yields are decent, but you have to account for the property management fees if you aren't local. Most agencies will take 20-30% of your gross rental income, which significantly changes your ROI calculation.
velvet_coder
Replied: 18.01.2026 06:20
Don't expect the same appreciation rates we saw five years ago. The market has cooled down slightly, but Sozopol remains a premium destination. If you love the vibe, buy it for yourself, not just as a financial vehicle.
saffron_sky
Replied: 03.02.2026 06:01
If you want peace and quiet, avoid the Old Town during July and August at all costs. The noise from the bars and foot traffic is relentless. If you are strictly an investor, look for properties in the Budjaka area instead—it’s much more serene.
