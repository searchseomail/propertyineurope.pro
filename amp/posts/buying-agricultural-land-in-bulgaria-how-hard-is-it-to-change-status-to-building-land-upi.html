Buying Agricultural Land in Bulgaria: How Hard Is It to Change Status to Building Land (UPI)?

silent_voyager OP
27.01.2025 07:51
I am currently looking at purchasing a plot of agricultural land in rural Bulgaria with the intention of building a small vacation home. I have heard conflicting information regarding the complexity and time required to reclassify this land into an Urbanized Plot (UPI). Could anyone share their recent experiences or offer advice on the bureaucratic hurdles involved in this process? I would especially appreciate insights on the average costs and the likelihood of approval for a plot located outside of village regulation limits.

It is definitely a long game. Changing land status from agricultural to UPI involves a PUPS (Detailed Development Plan) which can take anywhere from 12 to 24 months depending on the municipality. Make sure you have a local lawyer to handle the paperwork.

I tried this in a village near Veliko Tarnovo. The main hurdle is the soil category. If it's category 1-3, it is nearly impossible. If it's 4-10, you have a better chance, but still expect to pay thousands in surveyor fees and municipal taxes.
